## 2. Scientific Rationale and Mechanism of Action

Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus (T1DM) is an autoimmune disease driven by progressive autoreactive T-lymphocyte-mediated destruction of pancreatic β-cells. At clinical diagnosis, patients typically retain a residual functional β-cell mass capable of endogenous insulin secretion.

* **Mechanism of Action:** Inmunomab-D1 is a **non-mitogenic** humanized IgG1 monoclonal antibody targeting the CD3 complex on T lymphocytes. By inducing transient inactivation of autoreactive T cells and promoting regulatory T-cell (Treg) expansion, a single short 14-day treatment course aims to restore immune tolerance without inducing prolonged systemic immunosuppression, thereby preserving C-peptide secretion and reducing long-term exogenous insulin requirements and microvascular/macrovascular complications.

## 4. Clinical Operations, Regulatory, and Data Management Framework

The protocol synopsis defines core operational, ethical, and safety standards across the study lifecycle:

* **Good Clinical Practice & Ethics Approvals:** Conducted in strict compliance with **ICH-GCP** guidelines and the **Declaration of Helsinki**. No study procedure may be initiated prior to formal approval of the protocol and Informed Consent/Assent Forms (including Informed Assent for minors aged 12–17 years) by the Ethics Committee (**CEIm**) and Competent Regulatory Authority.
* **Risk-Based Monitoring (RBM):** Combines on-site monitoring visits with remote and centralized data review, prioritizing **100% Source Data Verification (SDV)** on critical parameters: inclusion/exclusion eligibility criteria, primary endpoint (**C-peptide**), and **Serious Adverse Events (SAEs)**.
* **Clinical Data Management:** Pseudoanonymized subject data are captured in a validated Electronic Case Report Form (**eCRF**) compliant with **FDA** and **EMA** requirements, enforcing a **5-business-day** data entry timeline after each participant visit.
* **Pharmacovigilance & Expedited Safety Reporting:** Investigators must notify any **SAE** to the Sponsor within a strict **24-hour** deadline from awareness. Suspected Unexpected Serious Adverse Reactions (**SUSARs**) are reported expeditiously to **EudraVigilance** and involved Ethics Committees (**CEIm**) within statutory timelines (**7 or 15 days** depending on fatality/life-threatening severity).
